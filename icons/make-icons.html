Scrollshot icon builder

Chrome only accepts raster icons, so render the logo to PNG here, then drop the files back into this icons/ folder.

  1. Click Download all PNGs — four files land in your Downloads folder.
  2. Move icon-16.png, icon-32.png, icon-48.png and icon-128.png into this icons/ folder.
  3. Rename manifest.chrome-with-icons.json to manifest.json (keep a copy of the old one), then reload the extension.
